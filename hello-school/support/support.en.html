Hello School Support

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

If you need help or would like to suggest an improvement, email us:
treegteam@icloud.com

What to include in your email

  • a brief description of the issue and the steps that lead to it;
  • your phone model;
  • your iOS or Android version;
  • your app version;
  • screenshots, if helpful.

Do not send your BilimClass password, sign-in tokens or parent code. Before sharing screenshots, hide personal information that is not needed to explain the issue.

Your BilimClass account

Hello School does not create a separate account. Your school and BilimClass manage access recovery, changes to school records and deletion of your BilimClass account.
